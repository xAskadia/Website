FAQ

Good to know.

How long until my website is live?

Depending on the package, between two and six weeks. We agree on the exact timeline in our first call – and I stick to it.

What do I need to prepare?

Almost nothing. Tell me about your business, send me your logo and any photos you have. We'll work out the rest together.

Do I have to write the copy?

No. I'll ask you targeted questions and turn your answers into copy that sounds like you and gets found on Google.

How much do domain and hosting cost?

Usually between €5 and €15 per month. I'll give you independent advice and set everything up for you.

Can I extend my website later?

Of course. Your website grows with your business – new pages, languages or features can be added at any time.

Do you only work in the Allgäu region?

My office is in Kempten, my clients are everywhere. We can meet in person or conveniently via video call – in German or English.
