About me

Hello, I'm Adelina.

Web designer from Kempten in the Allgäu. I believe every business has a story that deserves to be seen.

Design with heart and strategy.

I've been designing websites for more than six years. What began as a passion for beautiful design has become my calling: helping businesses become visible online.

Through my work in online marketing and as a project manager, I know what a website really has to achieve: build trust, get found and turn visitors into customers. That's exactly what I bring together in every project – creative design and clear strategy.

I especially care about trades, restaurants and small businesses that don't have a website yet. I take my time, explain things clearly and guide you personally – from the first coffee to launch day and beyond.
